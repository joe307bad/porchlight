Research date: 2026-11-08. ## 1. How games gate furniture/decor (currencies, mission rewards, collections, crafting, rotating shops, achievements, seasonal)

### Takeaway
The successful modern pattern is a mix of sources: earn decor through ordinary play (quests, achievements, reputation, raids, professions), buy some with soft currency from vendors, craft some, and add a thin premium layer. WoW Midnight (launched March 2026) is the clearest current case. It turned its 20-year achievement and quest catalog into decor rewards, so housing becomes a "collection" that hangs off existing progression.

### Cited Findings
- WoW Midnight decor comes from "quests, achievements, factions, vendors, raids, professions, and more". Example: the Onyxia head trophy is a reward for the "More DoTs! (25 Player)" achievement. — [Warcraft Tavern: Where to get decor](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow/news/where-to-get-player-housing-decor-in-world-of-warcraft-midnight/); [Icy Veins housing preview](https://wp-prod.icy-veins.com/?p=57562)
- WoW players got four themed exterior decor "kits" at launch along with a large decor catalog. Collecting every decor piece "has already become a new hobby for many WoW players" (search summary of Icy Veins coverage). — [Icy Veins Player Housing Guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/player-housing-guide)
- WoW housing early access opened in December 2025 for Midnight pre-orders, and the full launch was March 2, 2026. Players without the expansion can visit neighborhoods and collect decor from NPC quests in Orgrimmar, Stormwind, and Dornogal, but cannot claim a home. Collecting works as a funnel toward the purchase. — [IDC Games: Housing Early Access](https://idcgames.com/en/world-of-warcraft/news/azeroth-welcomes-you-home-as-housing-early-access-arrives-in-world-of-warcraft-midnight-2025-12-03-11-35-11979); [Escapist, Dec 2025](https://www.escapistmagazine.com/news-wow-player-housing-december-2025/)
- WoW has "house level" progression: interior and exterior maximum decor budgets rise with house level. One example is the exterior budget going from 200 to 250 at House Level 3 (Dec 2025). — [warcraft.wiki.gg API: GetAllMaxPlacementBudgets](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_C_HousingDecor.GetAllMaxPlacementBudgets); [Blizzard Watch, Dec 9 2025](https://blizzardwatch.com/2025/12/09/decorators-rejoiced-player-housing-exterior-budget-raised-250); [Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow/news/exterior-budget-increased-for-player-housing-in-world-of-warcraft/)

- WoW's Neighborhood "Endeavors" are monthly collaborative neighborhood-wide events. They were held back from early access until full launch. — [Icy Veins neighborhoods guide](https://astro.k.icy-veins.com/wow/player-housing-neighborhoods-guide)
- A July 2026 guide refers to "Decoration Duels" and new buildings ahead of the "Curse of Ula'tek" update. - Palia: furniture comes mainly from crafting (Furniture Making skill, mentor NPC Tish, a workbench on your plot). Other sources are villager gifts, treasure chests, and accomplishments. — [Palia wiki: Furniture Making](https://palia.wiki.gg/wiki/Furniture_Making/en); [Palia wiki: Decor](https://palia.wiki.gg/wiki/Decor/en); [GINX Palia housing guide](https://www.ginx.tv/en/?p=50548)
- Palia also has "Functional Furniture" (decor with gameplay use) and "Growable Furniture". — [Palia wiki: Functional Furniture](https://palia.wiki.gg/wiki/Functional_Furniture); [Palia wiki: Growable Furniture](https://palia.wiki.gg/wiki/Growable_Furniture)
- Palia plot expansion (housing slots) is gated by quests and story progression. — [Palian Life: Unlocking Housing Slots](https://palianlife.pages.dev/guides/quest/guide-unlocking-housing-slots-in-palia); [Paliapedia: Kenji housing plot value quest](https://paliapedia.com/quest/main-kenji-housingplotvalue)
- Animal Crossing ties decor gating to the "series" and "theme" structure: series items with matching wallpaper and flooring reward collecting a complete set. — [Prima Games New Leaf guide](https://primagames.com/eguides/animal-crossing-new-leaf-eguide/home-sweet-home/wowing-the-hha); [Thonky HHA (New Leaf)](https://www.thonky.com/animal-crossing-new-leaf/happy-home-academy)

### Inferences
- For Porchlight, mission types (delivery, chase, firefighting, races) map naturally onto WoW-style "decor as achievement trophy". Examples: a fire-helmet coat rack after N fires, or a trophy shelf for race medals. Each mission type would then produce a recognizable decor family that visitors can read as a record of play.
- A "house level" (budget grows with progression) gives a progression axis that does not depend on new items. It is cheap to build and lets the item catalog stay smaller.
- Letting non-owners and new players collect decor before they own anything worked as a funnel for WoW. Porchlight could let a player in a shared town collect items before they finish the house-claim step.

## 2. Evaluation/scoring systems (HHA, themes, Valheim comfort, Palia) and their effect on creativity

### Takeaway
There are two scoring philosophies. In Animal Crossing's HHA, scoring is aesthetic and judgmental: points for series, themes, feng shui colors, and item count. Players widely see it as optional and opaque, and guides say feng shui "isn't likely to make or break your ranking". In Valheim's Comfort, scoring is functional and coarse: distinct furniture categories within 10 m extend the Rested buff. The Valheim approach rewards building a home without dictating its look, because it counts variety of categories rather than style.

### Cited Findings
- HHA point values for feng shui vary by game. Wild World gives 100 points per correctly placed item, New Leaf 300 per proper-colored item in the proper zone, and New Horizons reportedly 500 per valid item, capped at 1,500 per room. — [Nookipedia: Fêng shui](https://nookipedia.com/wiki/F%C3%AAng_shui); [Thonky (New Leaf)](https://www.thonky.com/animal-crossing-new-leaf/happy-home-academy)
- Series bonus in New Leaf: with 4 or more unique items from the same series displayed, each scores 1,000 points. Wallpaper and flooring don't count toward the series bonus. — [GamerGuides New Leaf HHA](https://gamerguides.com/animal-crossing-new-leaf/guide/main-street/nooks-homes/happy-home-academy)
- Guides note that following feng shui "isn't likely going to make or break your ranking". One player experiment found no score difference (anecdotal). — [Mic: How to impress the HHA](https://www.mic.com/p/animal-crossing-new-horizons-how-to-improve-your-happy-home-score-22796233); [ACNH FAQ: HHA](https://chibisnorlax.github.io/acnhfaq/island-dev/hha)
- New Horizons rewards high HHA scores with trophies such as the Gold HHA Trophy. These are optional chase goals. — [Pro Game Guides: Gold HHA Trophy](https://progameguides.com/animal-crossing/how-to-get-a-gold-hha-trophy-in-animal-crossing-new-horizons/)
- Valheim: Rested duration = 7 minutes plus your Comfort level. Comfort lengthens the buff but does not strengthen it (50% health regen, 100% stamina regen, 50% XP gain). Furniture counts within about 10 m, and duplicates of the same category don't stack. Normal progression caps around Comfort 17, and seasonal items (Maypole, Yule Tree) can push it to 19. Being Wet blocks Rested. — [Mobalytics Valheim comfort guide](https://mobalytics.gg/gamebase/guides/valheim-comfort-rested-guide); [Shockbyte comfort guide](https://shockbyte.com/blog/simple-valheim-comfort-guide); [Corrosion Hour](https://www.corrosionhour.com/valheim-how-does-comfort-work/)
- Palia uses "functional furniture" (crafting stations, storage and so on) rather than an aesthetic score. — [Palia wiki: Functional Furniture](https://palia.wiki.gg/wiki/Functional_Furniture)
- WoW Midnight's "Decoration Duels" (July 2026) suggests player-judged or event-based decor competition rather than an automated score (unverified detail). — [PlayNews.gg](https://www.playnews.gg/en/guides/wow-midnight-housing-buildings-and-decoration-duels-before-the-curse-of-ula-tek-july-2026)

### Inferences
- An aesthetic auto-score (HHA-style) risks pushing everyone toward the same "optimal" rooms, because players fill rooms with matching series. Coarse category-based scores (Valheim) reward completeness without prescribing style. Porchlight could use a small Valheim-like "cozy" buff, for example a short mission bonus after resting at home. It would count distinct categories (seat, bed, light, table, plant) and not reward stacking duplicates.
- Seasonal items that add comfort (Valheim Maypole and Yule Tree) are a light way to make limited-time decor feel useful without being mandatory.
- Peer recognition (likes and recommendations, see section 3) may be a better "score" for creativity than an algorithmic rating.

### Gaps
- No primary data on whether HHA engagement correlates with retention.
- No developer statements (GDC or interviews) found on design intent behind HHA or Valheim comfort in this session.

## 3. Visiting and showcasing (dream addresses, home tours, neighborhoods, likes, permissions, co-op decorating, events)

### Takeaway
Showcasing has moved from friends-only visits to discoverable, curated directories. ESO Home Tours (Update 43, Aug 2024) offers Recommended, Browse, and Favorites tabs, with recommendations rate-limited to 10 per week. Animal Crossing's Dream Addresses offer asynchronous snapshot visits. WoW Midnight puts houses in persistent neighborhoods (public or guild) with monthly group Endeavors. Permissions are usually layered: privacy (private, friends, anyone), plus per-player editor roles for co-decorating (Palia).

### Cited Findings
- ESO Home Tours (Update 43, Aug 2024) has four tabs: Recommended, Browse Homes, Favorite Homes, and My Listings. Recommended mixes "the most consistently recommended homes as well as newer homes that haven't been showcased yet". — [UESP: Home Tours](https://content1.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Home_Tours); [Alcast Home Tours guide](https://alcasthq.com/eso-home-tours-guide/)
- ESO: each player can recommend up to 10 homes per week and favorite up to 100. You must be inside a home to recommend or favorite it. Achievements reward touring: "Home Tourist" (visit 3 recommended homes) and "Home Herald" (recommend 10 homes). — [UESP: Home Tours](https://content1.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Home_Tours); [ESO-Hub: Home Tours](https://eso-hub.com/en/ingame-help/home-tours)
- ESO forum feedback asked for a visit history, among other things. ESO-Hub covered player reception and post-launch improvements. — [ESO-Hub: reception of Home Tours](https://eso-hub.com/en/news/eso-player-reception-of-home-tours-feature-u43-improvements-and-updates); [ESO forums: Recommendations to improve Home Tours](https://forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/664724)
- ESO: a ZOS developer said guest count in a home affects performance, and that raising the visitor limit would require lowering furnishing limits (Feb 2020). — [ESO forums (ZOS post)](https://forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/comment/4069761/)
- WoW Midnight: two faction housing zones. Players are split into neighborhoods of "public" and "guild" types. Pre-order early access allowed one Horde and one Alliance neighborhood per Battle.net account. — [Icy Veins neighborhoods guide](https://astro.k.icy-veins.com/wow/player-housing-neighborhoods-guide); [IDC Games](https://idcgames.com/en/world-of-warcraft/news/azeroth-welcomes-you-home-as-housing-early-access-arrives-in-world-of-warcraft-midnight-2025-12-03-11-35-11979)
- Blizzard explicitly positioned WoW housing against FFXIV with "no lotteries and no onerous upkeep" (search result headline). — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/platforms/pc-gaming/world-of-warcraft-calls-out-final-fantasy-14s-house-lottery-nightmare-as-blizzard-details-midnights-player-housing-no-lotteries-and-no-onerous-upkeep)
- Palia: Plot Settings let owners choose a security mode (Private, Friends, Anyone) and add or remove per-player "editor" permissions for co-decorating. Visitors can interact (for example, sit on furniture) but cannot move furniture or building blocks. You visit friends through the social menu when their status reads "Housing Plot", and cannot visit when the owner is offline. — [Palia support: Customization](https://support.palia.com/hc/en-us/articles/7474242903444-Customization); [Siliconera: Visit other plots in Palia](https://www.siliconera.com/how-to-visit-other-plots-in-palia/); [AllThings.How](https://allthings.how/palia-how-to-visit-a-friend-s-plot/)
- Animal Crossing Dream Addresses: uploaded snapshot islands that anyone can visit asynchronously. The uploader's bulletin board and comment text are visible, and both are subject to moderation (see section 5). — [Animal Crossing World: 4 things before uploading dreams](https://animalcrossingworld.com/2020/07/4-things-to-know-before-uploading-animal-crossing-dreams-advertising-cheating-bulletin-board-ladders/)

### Inferences
- Porchlight already has houses in a shared town, which works like a WoW-style neighborhood. Adding an ESO-style "recommend" (rate-limited, for example a few per week, given from inside the house) and a town-level "featured homes" rotation would make discovery work without leaderboards becoming toxic.
- Palia's offline-owner restriction is a limitation. Porchlight's persistent town could let visitors enter homes when the owner is offline (read-only), which ACNH approximates with Dream snapshots.
- Rewarding visitors (ESO achievements for touring and recommending) ties showcasing into progression for the visitor too. Example: a "visit 5 neighbors' homes" daily mission alongside deliveries.
- Permissions model to copy: house privacy (private, friends, town, anyone) plus explicit per-player "co-decorator" grants, with visitors able to sit and interact but not move items.

### Gaps
- No verified data on FFXIV-specific visitor features (estate profile and greeting text, guestbook-like "estate greeting") in this session.
- No found data on photo modes tied to housing, or on likes/guestbook features beyond ESO recommendations.
- No found data on parties or events hosted at homes (in-game tooling), beyond community practice.

## 4. Housing as retention driver: evidence

### Takeaway
Hard retention data is scarce publicly. Demand signals are strong, though. FFXIV's housing scarcity has been a multi-year crisis, with plots selling out in hours and a lottery added in Patch 6.1. Housing-centric Roblox games have large lifetimes: Bloxburg at about 10B visits with an all-time peak of about 592K concurrent players (CCU), Adopt Me! over 45B visits by Oct 2026. WoW's housing reveal reportedly drew record social engagement. I found no published WoW retention numbers attributable to housing.

### Cited Findings
- FFXIV: in a 2016 patch, new affordable plots sold out within hours. Patch 6.1 (2022) introduced a lottery and the Empyreum district, adding 1,440 plots per server. Of the Ishgard plots, only 360 were open to individuals and 1,080 were restricted to Free Companies. Critics noted FC wards sat mostly empty while private wards were full. — [Inverse](https://www.inverse.com/input/gaming/final-fantasy-14-housing-lottery-technical-issues); [RPGSite](https://www.rpgsite.net/feature/12871-final-fantasy-xivs-housing-situation-is-still-a-depressing-mess-and-it-deserves-better)
- FFXIV's lottery had bugs: plots showing no entrants and winning numbers of 0. Producer Naoki Yoshida apologized and outlined fixes. — [mein-mmo](https://mein-mmo.de/en/final-fantasy-xiv-everyone-wants-to-win-a-spot-for-their-house-but-the-lottery-is-so-broken-that-the-boss-intervenes,813235); [Massively OP](https://massivelyop.com/?p=135522); [PC Gamer: FFXIV fixing housing crisis](https://pcgamer.com/final-fantasy-14-is-desperately-trying-to-fix-its-housing-crisis)
- FFXIV reports describe a secondary market with houses reportedly costing about 1 billion gil (German press). — [mein-mmo: housing market](https://mein-mmo.de/en/housing-markt-in-final-fantasy-xiv,183845)
- Welcome to Bloxburg (Roblox, created 2014): about 9.9 to 10.1 billion lifetime visits. 14-day average CCU about 24.7K, all-time peak CCU about 592.5K (Rotrends). Average session about 24 minutes. CCU snapshots range from 14K to 33K depending on date. A RoWatcher article frames it as declined from peak. Third-party trackers, figures as of mid/late 2026. — [Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/88070565/Welcome-to-Bloxburg); [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/88070565/welcome-to-bloxburg); [RoWatcher: Bloxburg at 14K players](https://rowatcher.com/news/welcome-to-bloxburg-at-14k-players-can-roblox-s-building-icon-stage-a-comeback)
- Adopt Me! (pet and home roleplay; housing is a core loop): over 45 billion visits as of October 2026 per Wikipedia (about 41B in November 2025). Monetization includes housing upgrades among cosmetics, pets, and seasonal passes. A third-party revenue estimate of about $3.4M/month is low-confidence. — [Wikipedia: Adopt Me!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adopt_Me!); [RoWatcher: Adopt Me](https://rowatcher.com/games/383310974/adopt-me)
- WoW: one source claims the official housing announcement drew more social engagement than any WoW feature reveal since Classic (claim from search summary; primary not verified). — [AccountShark deep dive](https://accountshark.net/blog/wow-player-housing-midnight-deep-dive-biggest-feature) (low-reliability commercial source)

### Inferences
- The FFXIV lesson is that scarcity makes housing a status symbol but alienates the majority. Guaranteeing every player a house (Porchlight already does) avoids the biggest documented pain point. FFXIV's empty FC wards also warn against reserving capacity for group types that players don't use.
- Roblox numbers show housing and roleplay sustains huge multi-year audiences, but those games bundle housing with roleplay jobs. That matches Porchlight's missions-plus-home structure.

### Gaps
- No public D1/D7/D30 retention data attributable to housing in any title.
- No Blizzard-published engagement metrics for housing (decor collected, neighborhoods created).
- No hard ESO housing popularity or sales data found.

## 5. Moderation and griefing in user-created spaces

### Takeaway
Platform holders moderate shared, discoverable spaces mostly reactively. Nintendo removes Dream Addresses for hacked items, sexual content, and advertising (URLs or social handles in comments), sometimes years late. Edit permissions are owner-granted to prevent in-home griefing (Palia visitors cannot move items). Free-text surfaces (bulletin boards, comments, signs) are the highest-risk vector.

### Cited Findings
- January 2026: Nintendo deleted the Dream Address of a notorious Japanese "adults only" ACNH island that had been public since 2020, around the Switch 2 update. The creator thanked Nintendo "for turning a blind eye all these years". Nintendo's terms prohibit sexually explicit content, including content implied through context. — [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/nintendo-bans-infamous-japanese-adult-only-animal-crossing-island-creator-says-thanks-for-turning-a-blind-eye-all-these-years/); [Nintendo Life, Jan 2026](https://nintendolife.com/news/2026/01/random-an-adults-only-island-has-been-deleted-from-animal-crossing-new-horizons); [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/animal-crossing-adults-only-island-banned-by-nintendo-2000660187)
- 2020: Nintendo removed Dream islands containing hacked items. Others were removed for advertising, such as a blog URL or an Instagram username in the dream comment. Inappropriate bulletin board messages can also get a dream removed. — [GoNintendo](https://www.gonintendo.com/stories/366077-nintendo-removing-hacked-dream-islands-from-animal-crossing-new); [Animal Crossing World](https://animalcrossingworld.com/2020/07/4-things-to-know-before-uploading-animal-crossing-dreams-advertising-cheating-bulletin-board-ladders/)
- WoW Midnight: French press (JudgeHype) covered "suggestive" decorations created by players with housing, which shows that crude builds appear quickly even with a fixed item catalog. — [JudgeHype](https://worldofwarcraft.judgehype.com/news/housing-creativite-elfes-de-la-nuit-et-decorations-heu-suggestives-183147/)
- Palia: visitors cannot move or remove furniture. Only owner-designated editors can. — [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/how-to-visit-other-plots-in-palia/); [Palia support](https://support.palia.com/hc/en-us/articles/7474242903444-Customization)

### Inferences
- Even with no free text, item arrangements can be crude (WoW). For a small team: (1) report-a-house button feeding a review queue; (2) "hide this house" for the reporting player; (3) delist from public showcase on reports, before any ban; (4) filtered or preset text on signs and house names, with no URLs; (5) visitors never get edit rights by default.
- In shared towns, the exterior is visible to everyone, unlike opt-in interiors. Exteriors need stricter limits (fewer free-placement items, preset signage) than interiors.

### Gaps
- No documented FFXIV or ESO enforcement cases or policies on offensive housing found in this session.
- No data on item-spam griefing (for example, dropping items in others' yards). Most games simply don't allow placing on others' land.

## 6. Monetization patterns and backlash

### Takeaway
Cosmetic housing shops are standard (ESO Crown Store homes, WoW Hearthsteel). Backlash consistently clusters around three things: (a) item prices out of line with perceived value, (b) broken promises about what will be sold, (c) subscription perks that gate capacity (ESO Plus doubles furnishing limits). WoW's 2026 housing shop is the main current backlash case, and Blizzard cut some prices within days.

### Cited Findings
- WoW introduced Hearthsteel, its first premium currency, purchased with Battle.net balance and spent on housing decor. — [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/108776/world-of-warcrafts-new-second-paid-currency-can-be-spent-on-midnight-custom-housing/index.html); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/world-of-warcraft/it-sure-looks-like-world-of-warcraft-is-getting-its-first-premium-currency-after-21-years-to-monetize-player-housing-in-its-next-expansion)
- March 2026: a single Spring Blossom Tree priced at $7.50 caused outrage, and Blizzard discounted it (reported up to 66% off). Bundles of up to $25 (2,500 Hearthsteel) remained, and "the majority of housing decor [is] still earnable in-game". A Reddit post calling prices absurd drew about 2,000 upvotes. — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/world-of-warcraft/what-the-f-are-these-prices-world-of-warcraft-fans-question-blizzards-usd7-50-trees-prompting-swift-discounts-but-some-say-the-mmos-new-housing-decor-is-still-comically-overpriced); [mein-mmo: price drops 66%](https://mein-mmo.de/en/wow-introduces-new-cash-items-community-escalates-and-the-price-drops-immediately-by-66,1555358); [Icy Veins: housing store debate](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/news/beautiful-but-too-expensive-wow-housing-store-debate-reignites/)
- A later report says complaints led Blizzard to cut housing costs by up to 93% (date and scope unclear). — [mein-mmo](https://mein-mmo.de/en/complaining-helped-wow-reduces-housing-costs-by-up-to-93,1556495)
- Blizzard's November 2025 post said race- or class-tied items, or items already existing in Azeroth, wouldn't be sold. Windows Central says the shop later did exactly that. October 2026 Kul Tiran housing packs ($35) and a $75 Cozy Treehouse bundle drew fresh criticism. — [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/blizzard/blizzard-said-it-wouldnt-sell-real-money-world-of-warcraft-housing-items-tied-to-player-races-it-lied); [GamingHQ, Oct 29 2026](https://gaminghq.eu/2026/10/29/wow-kul-tiran-housing-packs-blizzard-backlash/); [Blizzard forums: "cash register"](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/blizzard-you-turned-player-housing-into-a-cash-register/2344065)
- ESO: furnishing limits are doubled for ESO Plus subscribers. Players complain that large Crown Store homes have the same cap as smaller manors. — [ArzyelBuilds ESO Housing](https://arzyelbuilds.com/eso-housing/); [ESO forums: Furnishing limit and Crown Store](https://forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/comment/4920835/)

### Inferences
- If Porchlight monetizes, sell cosmetic decor that has no in-world identity or progression meaning. Keep mission and achievement trophies non-purchasable, and never sell capacity (item caps) or plots. Price individual decor low. Write the "what we won't sell" promise down and keep it, because breaking it is what produced WoW's biggest backlash.

### Gaps
- No primary data on revenue share from housing in any title.

## 7. Inventory/storage limits and item caps, and rationale

### Takeaway
Every game caps placed items, and the stated reason is performance: rendering, network sync, and guest counts. Caps vary widely: ESO 700 (doubled with ESO Plus), Palia 3,500 per plot, WoW a weighted point budget where items cost 1/3/5. Weighted budgets tied to progression (WoW) turn a technical limit into a progression reward. The most frequent player request is "raise the cap".

### Cited Findings
- ESO: the furnishing limit is 700 for large homes, even oversized Crown Store homes (player-reported 2025/26). ESO Plus doubles item limits but not the player cap. ZOS says raising the cap is "one of the most frequent requests" and that guest count and furnishing count trade off for performance. — [ESO forums](https://forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/comment/8063588/); [ESO forums: ZOS on limits](https://forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/comment/4069761/); [UESP: Furniture](https://content1.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Furniture)
- Palia: maximum 3,500 decor items placed on a single housing plot. — [Palia wiki: Housing Plot](https://palia.wiki.gg/wiki/Housing_Plot)
- WoW: each decor item costs 1, 3, or 5 budget points against a house maximum. Interior and exterior budgets are separate and rise with house level. Reported changes: exterior 200 to 250 (Dec 2025). Patch 12.0.7 PTR increased decor limits and restored outdoor lighting. A beta datamine had Renown 7 and 9 each adding 500 interior points and 10 room slots. ### Inferences
- For a low-poly Unity town with many houses visible at once, use a weighted budget (big items cost more) with separate exterior and interior pools. Make the exterior pool smaller, since everyone in town renders it, and grow both pools with mission progression. Ship generous from day one. WoW and ESO show that "raise the cap" is the perennial complaint, and that a cap smaller than the developer's own showcase builds looks bad.
- Store owned decor as an unlimited account collection (WoW Housing Storage, ACNH catalog) and limit only placement. This avoids inventory-management friction.
